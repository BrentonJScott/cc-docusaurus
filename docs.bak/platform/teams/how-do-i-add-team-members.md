# Add Team Members

Invite people to a Team by email. When you finish, they appear in the Team member list.

## Invite members

1. **Open the Team**

   Open the Team you want to grow.

2. **Add members**

   Click **+ (Add Member)**. Enter each person's email address.

3. **Send invitations**

   Click **Invite Team Members**. Invited members appear in the member list with a pending status until they accept.

## Troubleshooting

<details>
<summary>The invitation email never arrives</summary>

Check spam folders. Confirm the email address. The invitee must create a Code Capsules account with that email to accept.

</details>
