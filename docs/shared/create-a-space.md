# Create a Space

Create a Space in your Team so you have somewhere to deploy capsules.

## Prerequisites

<DocCardList
  items={[
    {type: 'link', href: '/docs/shared/create-an-account', label: '👤 Code Capsules account', description: 'Sign up and open your Personal Team.'},
  ]}
/>

## Create your Space

1. **Start a new Space**

   On the dashboard, click **+** and select **New Space**.

2. **Enter the Space details**

   On **Create Space**, set:
   - **Team**: the Team to deploy into, such as your Personal Team.
   - **Name**: a name for the project.
   - **Region**: the region closest to your users.

3. **Create the Space**

   Click **Create Space**. The new Space opens and is ready for capsules.

## Troubleshooting

<details>
<summary>The Team you want isn't in the Team dropdown</summary>

You aren't a member of that Team. Ask a Team owner to add you, then try again.

</details>
