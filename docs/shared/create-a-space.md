# Create a Space

Create a Space to deploy your capsules into.

## Prerequisites

<DocCardList
  items={[
    {type: 'link', href: '/docs/shared/create-an-account', label: '👤 Code Capsules account', description: 'Sign up and open your Personal Team.'},
  ]}
/>

## Create your Space

1. **Start a new Space**

   On your Personal Team dashboard, create a new Space.

2. **Name the Space**

   Enter a name for the Space and save it. The Space opens, ready for capsules.

## Troubleshooting

<details>
<summary>You can't find where to create a Space</summary>

You must be on your Team's dashboard. Open your **Personal Team** and try again.

</details>
