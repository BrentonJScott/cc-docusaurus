# Create a Space

<Description />

## Prerequisites

<LinkCards>
  <LinkCard title="Code Capsules account" to="/docs/shared/create-an-account" icon="account">
    Sign up and reach your Personal Team.
  </LinkCard>
</LinkCards>

## Create your Space

<Steps>

1. **Open Spaces**

   Log in to Code Capsules and click the **Spaces** tab.

2. **Start a new Space**

   Click **New Space**.

3. **Choose a Team**

   Choose **Personal Team** if you're working alone, or a named Team if you're collaborating.

4. **Name the Space**

   Enter a name that reminds you of the project, such as `customer-api`.

5. **Choose a region**

   Choose a region close to most of your users.

6. **Create the Space**

   Click **Create Space**.

</Steps>

## Troubleshooting

<Accordion>

<details>
<summary>Your Team isn't in the list</summary>

You're not a member of that Team yet. Ask a Team owner to add you, or choose **Personal Team**.

</details>

</Accordion>
