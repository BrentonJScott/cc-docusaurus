# Create a Code Capsules Account

<Description />

## Prerequisites

You don't need anything before you start.

## Create your account

<Steps>

1. **Sign up**

   Open [Code Capsules](https://www.codecapsules.io/) and click **Sign Up**.

2. **Enter your details**

   Enter your details and create the account.

3. **Open your Personal Team**

   On your first login, click **Go To Personal Team**. Code Capsules gives every account a **Personal Team**, where you manage your Spaces and Capsules.

</Steps>

## Troubleshooting

<Accordion>

<details>
<summary>You already have an account</summary>

You don't need a new one. Click **Log in** instead of **Sign Up**, and use the same account across guides.

</details>

</Accordion>
