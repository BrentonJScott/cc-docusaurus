# Find and Download Your Invoices

Open your Team invoices in the dashboard. When you finish, invoices are listed on the **Invoices** tab.

## Prerequisites

<DocCardList
  items={[
    {type: 'link', href: '/docs/shared/create-an-account', label: '👤 Code Capsules account', description: 'Sign up and open your Personal Team.'},
  ]}
/>

## View invoices

1. **Open Billing**

   Click **Billing** in the top navigation bar.

2. **Open Invoices**

   Open the **Invoices** tab.

3. **Review the list**

   Each invoice shows its date, amount, and status. Open or download an invoice to view the PDF.
