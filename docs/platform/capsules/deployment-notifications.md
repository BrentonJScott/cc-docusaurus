# Set Up Deployment Notifications

Save alert and notification preferences for your Capsules. When you finish, your choices are stored after you click **Save Preferences**.

## Prerequisites

<DocCardList
  items={[
    {type: 'link', href: '/docs/shared/create-an-account', label: '👤 Code Capsules account', description: 'Sign up and open your Personal Team.'},
  ]}
/>

## Save notification preferences

1. **Open notification settings**

   Open **Alerting** or **Notifications** from the dashboard menu (wording depends on your build).

2. **Choose notification types**

   Toggle in-app and email alerts for events such as **Capsule Not Running**, **High CPU Usage**, and **High Memory Usage**.

3. **Save**

   Click **Save Preferences**. Reload the page; your toggles match what you saved.

## Troubleshooting

<details>
<summary>You do not receive email alerts</summary>

Enable email notifications for each event type and confirm your account email on the **Account** tab.

</details>
