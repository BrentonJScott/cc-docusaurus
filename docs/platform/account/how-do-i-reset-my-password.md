# Reset Your Password

Change your Code Capsules password. When you finish, you can log in with the new password.

## Prerequisites

<DocCardList
  items={[
    {type: 'link', href: '/docs/shared/create-an-account', label: '👤 Code Capsules account', description: 'Sign up and open your Personal Team.'},
  ]}
/>

## Change your password

1. **Open Account**

   Open the **Account** tab in the dashboard.

2. **Open password settings**

   In **Security**, click **Change Password**.

3. **Set a new password**

   Enter your current password, your new password, and confirmation. Save. Log out and log in with the new password to confirm.
