# Host Your Persistent Storage Capsule

Create a Persistent Storage Capsule on Code Capsules. When you finish, the storage capsule is running in your Space.

## Deploy your Persistent Storage capsule

1. **Open the capsule wizard**

   Open your Space. Select **New Capsule**.

2. **Choose Persistent Storage**

   On **Capsule Details**, select **Persistent Storage**, your Team, and Space. Click **Next**.

3. **Choose a plan**

   On **Plan**, select **Free**. Click **Next**.

4. **Create the capsule**

   On **Plan**, click **Create Capsule**.

5. **Confirm the capsule is running**

   On your **Space** dashboard, the Persistent Storage Capsule shows as running.
